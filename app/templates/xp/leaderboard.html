How to read this board

  • Levels reflect accumulated verified contribution, nothing else.
  • A level never grants permissions, pay, project ownership or authority over a colleague.
  • Categories are kept separate because a single score would misrepresent the work.
  • XP cannot be earned by signing in, chatting or running a timer without finishing it.
  • The CEO can hide this board entirely while keeping everyone's own progress visible.
